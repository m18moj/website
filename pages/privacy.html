Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide directly, such as email, name, and billing country during checkout. This is a demo application storing data locally in your browser—no information is sent to external servers.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your information is used solely for processing orders and maintaining your account. We do not share data with third parties. In this prototype, all data is stored locally in localStorage.

3. Data Security

This is a demonstration prototype. Passwords are hashed locally using a simple algorithm (not production-grade). For real applications, use proper encryption and secure servers.

4. Cookies and Tracking

We do not use cookies or tracking. This app uses browser localStorage for basket and session management.

5. Your Rights

You can delete your basket and account data at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage or using your browser's dev tools to manage stored data.

6. Contact

For questions about privacy, open a ticket on the discord server with the tag "privacy".

7. Discord Bot

The ScripForge Discord bot is a separate service with its own terms of service. Please refer to the Discord Bot Terms of Service and/or the Discord Bot Privacy Policy for details.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary for the purposes outlined in this policy. You can delete your data at any time by clearing your browser's localStorage.

9. Updates to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page.
