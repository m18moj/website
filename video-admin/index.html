Trend Intelligence

                        

                            
                            
                            
                        

                    

                    
Runs automatically in the background — trend signal from YouTube, Roblox-specific search, and Reddit every hour, performance stats every 6h, learned insights + trend forecasts + reward/punish grading nightly (see social/scheduler.js) — and feeds straight back into how future campaigns are planned. The breakdown below is the same signal, applied to the render controls on Pack Renders: it's the difference between guessing at a video type and knowing which one actually performs.

Merges momentum direction with forecast confidence to surface the trends most worth acting on now. Topics pinned as "always pursue" by an admin float to the top; blocklisted topics are hidden.

Trend alerts — high-confidence rising signals

                        

                        
When a rising trend crosses both a momentum and forecast-confidence threshold, it surfaces here. If a webhook URL is configured (TREND_ALERT_WEBHOOK_URL), alerts are also posted to Discord/Slack.

                        
Loading…

                    


                    

                        

                            
Trend forecasts — what's likely to happen next

                        

                        
Nightly, the AI predicts where each trend source is headed over the next week, with a confidence score, grounded in real momentum data and whether trend-jacking has actually paid off in past campaign performance (see social/agents/trendForecastAgent.js). Once a forecast's horizon passes, it's graded against what really happened.

                        
Loading…

                    


                    

                        

                            
AI track record — reward when right, punish when wrong

                        

                        
Every prediction — a trend forecast or a video's popularity score — is graded against reality once the outcome is known, and the result (confidence-weighted reward or penalty) is fed straight back into that agent's next prompt. This is its accuracy so far.
